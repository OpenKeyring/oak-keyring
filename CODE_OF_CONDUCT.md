# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want oak-keyring to be a respectful, practical, and security-conscious open-source project. Participants are expected to:

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying clear about technical risk.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Avoid harassment, insults, personal attacks, and private disclosure of others' information.
- Avoid posting secrets, passwords, vault files, recovery words, tokens, or private logs.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, discussions, or contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately by email at alphaqiu@gmail.com.
